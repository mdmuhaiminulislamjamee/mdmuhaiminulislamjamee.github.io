Md. Muhaiminul Islam (Jamee)

• Research Scholar • Embedded Systems & Robotic System Developer • Deep Learning, IoT & Python Specialist • Industrial Automation Expert

Research Interest: Autonomous Vehicles, Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, Robotics, IoT, Control Systems, Embedded Systems, SLAM, Digital Image & Signal Processing, and Intelligent Systems.

I am an Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering graduate from Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET), Bangladesh. My work sits at the intersection of embedded systems, robotics, artificial intelligence, and industrial automation, with a particular interest in building intelligent systems that can sense, reason, and act in real-world environments.

During my undergraduate studies, I developed more than 25 practical projects spanning IoT, computer vision, robotics, web development, and machine learning. My research portfolio includes six published IEEE conference papers and three manuscripts under review, with work on autonomous waste management, intelligent monitoring, smart notice systems, unmanned surveillance, regenerative braking, and communication systems. I served as an Undergraduate Research Assistant in the Department of Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering at RUET from 2023 to 2025.

My engineering experience covers embedded C/C++, microcontrollers, PCB and mixed-signal design, sensor integration, communication protocols, TinyML, edge AI, ROS 2, and deep-learning workflows. Alongside research and development, I have contributed to teaching, technical training, mentoring, and academic programs in robotics, mathematics, science, and engineering.

My long-term goal is to pursue a PhD and contribute to research in AI, SLAM, robotics, autonomous systems, and embedded intelligence. I hope to develop rigorous, resource-efficient technologies with practical social and industrial impact while continuing to grow as a researcher, educator, and technical leader.
